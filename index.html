Contourvolution

Contour lines and hillshade generated entirely in the browser, per tile, as vector geometry — no pre-rendered rasters. Elevation data from Mapterhorn.

Try the two experimental contour modes in the panel →

Shadow Density — line density encodes terrain steepness: more lines in shadow, fewer in light.
Minimum Spacing — contours are drawn in priority order (by elevation subdivision) and masked wherever they crowd together.
